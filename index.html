HOW IT WORKS

From a pile of receipts to one wallet tap.

Everything is computed on your device from integer satoshis. The only time money moves is when you approve a transaction in your own wallet.

  1. 1

    Log what was spent

    Add each expense, who paid and who shared it. Costs split evenly in satoshis: everyone gets the floor share and the leftover satoshis go to the first people in the list, so nothing is lost to rounding.

  2. 2

    Net it all out

    TabCash totals what each person paid against what they owe. Positive means the group owes you; negative means you owe the group.

  3. 3

    Shrink the paybacks

    The settlement planner matches debtors to creditors so a short list of transfers clears the whole tab, instead of everyone paying everyone.

  4. 4

    Pay in BCH, directly

    Connect a WizardConnect wallet and approve each payment yourself. Funds go wallet-to-wallet. You then check the transaction and record the settlement.

Start here · a two-minute TabCash walkthrough

TabCash opens in My tab: an empty workspace that stays in this browser and uses real BCH mainnet. Once a wallet is connected you can also open a separate Sample demo, which never sends BCH.

  1. Add your group and record your first shared expense.
  2. Connect a wallet to unlock payments. After connecting you can switch between My tab and the Sample demo; they keep separate data.
  3. In My tab, add each recipient's real BCH address and review the settlement plan.
  4. Wallet payments are real BCH mainnet transactions. Confirm the recipient, amount, fee and change in your wallet; verify the transaction before recording a settlement.
