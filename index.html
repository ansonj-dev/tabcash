How it works

From a pile of receipts to one wallet tap.

Everything is computed on your device in whole satoshis. Money moves only when you approve a transaction in your own wallet.

  1. 1

    Connect your wallet

    Pair a WizardConnect HD wallet on BCH mainnet. TabCash never sees a private key.

  2. 2

    Log what was spent

    Add people and expenses. Each cost splits evenly in satoshis, with leftover satoshis assigned in order, so nothing is lost to rounding.

  3. 3

    Net it all out

    Totals what each person paid against what they owe. A plus sign means the group owes you; a minus sign means you owe the group.

  4. 4

    Pay in BCH, directly

    A short list of paybacks clears the tab. You approve each payment in your wallet, then record the settlement.

Trust

Your keys, your coins, your device.

Payments are real BCH mainnet transactions, confirmed in your wallet. TabCash is only the ledger and the messenger.

Non-custodial

No keys are collected and no funds are held. Your wallet signs; TabCash cannot spend.

Local-first

Your tab lives in this browser’s storage. Export it as JSON whenever you like.

Guard-railed

A mainnet consent check, payments between 546 and 1,000,000 satoshis (0.01 BCH), and a fixed 1,000 satoshi fee.
